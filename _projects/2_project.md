**Status: dissertation chapter, in progress**

A typology of blame-attribution strategies — including attribution to political opponents, external causes, and self-blame — paired with a fine-tuned open-source large language model pipeline for classifying parliamentary speech at scale.

Applied across eight cases spanning parliamentary and presidential systems: Germany, Greece, Spain, Sweden, Brazil, Chile, Mexico, and two U.S. states.
