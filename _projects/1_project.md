**Status: working paper**

A difference-in-differences analysis of how German state governing parties attribute blame after natural disasters — externalizing, internalizing, credit-claiming, or staying neutral — and how that choice relates to their vote share at the next state election.

Blame strategies are coded from parliamentary speech using a large language model classification pipeline, then linked to a panel of state elections, disaster events, and economic conditions.
